# Colour clash

An open book in 8-bit pixel art on an attribute grid, one ink and one paper to each 8 × 8 cell: yellow pages with black lines of text on a blue cover, and a red ribbon whose cells turn the text beside it red; the cells either side of the gutter lose their bright bit, shading the pages in blocks.

**Book:** open book. **Style first used:** [Fire button](../medialog-logos-05/08-demo-08.md), collection 05.
