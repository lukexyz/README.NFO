README.NFO / MEDIALOG / COMPACT IDENTITIES 07

The media set, rebound as books.

The ten styles of collection 05, each redrawn around a book with fewer details and no tile or border: a fluorescent-display book, a hardback with a test-card cover, a jukebox menu as a contents page, a pinball dot-matrix book, a stack of books with keyboard page edges, a chrome book with a twister spine, a book traced by a light synth, a pixel book with attribute clash, an early home-computer title card and a swap slip tucked into a hardback. One plain wordmark. Your navy: #18223a. Every preview starts at the screenshot’s actual size: 203 × 74 pixels. All static.
