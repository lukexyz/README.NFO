# Glass book

A translucent open book turns slowly in space. Where its pages overlap, the glass deepens, like a glenz object from a nineties demo.

**Motion:** The book turns once every sixteen seconds and two small glints pulse; the static file holds a three-quarter view. The [static SVG](assets/04-pc-13-still.svg) holds the resting pose.
