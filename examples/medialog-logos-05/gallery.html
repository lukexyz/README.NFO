README.NFO / MEDIALOG / COMPACT IDENTITIES 05

Watched. Read. Played. Heard.

Ten styles never used for MediaLog, each aimed at a kind of media: a video recorder’s readout and a cracktro clapperboard for films, a test card for TV, a jukebox and a synth screen for albums, a pinball display and an 8-bit joystick for games, a light-synth microphone for podcasts, crack-screen panels for books, and a tick-box slip for all three. One plain wordmark. Your navy: #18223a. Every preview starts at the screenshot’s actual size: 203 × 74 pixels. All static.
