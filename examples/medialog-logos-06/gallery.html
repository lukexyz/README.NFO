Ten ways to draw a book.

Ten favourite graphic styles from earlier MediaLog collections, each redrawn around a book with fewer details and, mostly, no tile or border: a chrome hardback, a synthwave sunset sinking into the pages, a glass book, the Amiga insert-disk hand, a pencil sketch, a glossy Web 2.0 edition, a copper-bar bookshelf, a book made of 3D pipes, a 1-bit Mac icon and a Memphis paperback. One plain wordmark. Your navy: #18223a. Every preview starts at the screenshot’s actual size: 203 × 74 pixels. All static.
